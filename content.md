## High-Profile Victims Killed in Gaza

- **Hind Rajab (5 years old):** The Palestinian girl honored by Houston Texans linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair on his eye black in September 2026. She was killed in Gaza City in January 2024 after being trapped in a car under fire and making a widely publicized, hours-long emergency call to the Palestinian Red Crescent.   
- **Fatima Al-Bayook (8 years old):** Another young girl honored by Al-Shaair on his eye black in October 2026. She had survived an earlier airstrike that killed her family in 2023 before later being killed in Gaza.
- **Khaled Nabhan and Reem Nabhan (3 years old):** Khaled Nabhan became widely known internationally as the "soul of my soul" grandfather after footage of him mourning his 3-year-old granddaughter Reem—killed in an Israeli attack in November 2023—went viral. Khaled Nabhan himself was killed in an Israeli strike in December 2024.   
- **Refaat Alareer (44 years old):** A prominent Palestinian poet, writer, and professor of English literature at the Islamic University of Gaza, best known for his poem *"If I must die"*. He was killed in an Israeli airstrike in Gaza City on December 6, 2023, alongside several members of his family.   
- **Sha'ban al-Dalou (19 years old):** A software engineering student who burned to death on October 14, 2024, after an Israeli strike hit a tent encampment at Shuhada al-Aqsa Hospital in Deir al-Balah. Video footage of him trapped in the fire drew widespread international coverage; his mother and two younger siblings also died from the attack.   
- **Hamza Al-Dahdouh:** An Al Jazeera journalist and the son of Al Jazeera's Gaza bureau chief Wael Al-Dahdouh. He was killed in an Israeli airstrike in southern Gaza on January 7, 2024, alongside freelance journalist **Mustafa Thuria**, becoming the fifth member of Wael Al-Dahdouh's immediate family killed during the war.   

## People Killed by ICE Agents

- **Renée Nicole Good:** A U.S. citizen and mother shot and killed by a masked ICE agent in Minneapolis on January 7, 2026, while behind the wheel of her vehicle. Agents claimed she attempted to ram them, while eyewitness accounts and video footage sparked nationwide protests and calls for a federal investigation.   
- **Keith Porter Jr. (43 years old):** A U.S. citizen and father of two shot and killed by an off-duty ICE agent outside his home in Northridge, Los Angeles, on New Year's Eve 2025. The agent alleged Porter fired at him, while Porter's family stated he was firing celebratory shots and posed no threat.   
- **Silverio Villegas González (38 years old):** A Mexican national, cook, and father of two shot and killed by an ICE agent during a traffic stop in the Chicago suburb of Franklin Park on September 12, 2025. DHS claimed he hit and dragged an officer with his car, though video analysis by *The New York Times* and footage obtained by the *Chicago Sun-Times* contradicted the agency's account.   
- **Lorenzo Salgado Araujo:** A Mexican national shot and killed by ICE agents after a traffic stop; ICE alleged he tried to ram agents with his car, a claim contradicted by three witnesses.
- **Joan Sebastian Guerrero (26 years old):** A Colombian national shot and killed by an ICE agent on July 10, 2026, with ICE stating the agent opened fire as Guerrero attempted to drive away.
- **Deaths in ICE Custody:** Beyond fatal shootings, human rights monitors and news outlets have also documented a sharp rise in deaths inside ICE detention facilities. One widely reported case is **Mr. Lunas Campos**, who died at Camp East Montana in El Paso, Texas, in January 2026; ICE initially cited a medical emergency, but the El Paso County Medical Examiner ruled his death a homicide resulting from asphyxia due to neck and torso compression. The American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA) maintains a public log of all reported ICE custody deaths.
